# Purpose

Enforces the cash-account rule. A SELL on a symbol with no held long
position would create a new short entry and is not permitted in a cash
account.

# Account policy (Jun, 2026-10-28 clarification)

* **SIMULATE permits margin buying and short selling. REAL remains cash-only.**
* `lib/account-type.js` enables margin only for the explicit pair
  `TRD_ENV=SIMULATE` + `ACCOUNT_TYPE=margin`. Whitespace and case are normalised.
* `TRD_ENV=REAL`, or an unset, empty or unrecognised `TRD_ENV`, resolves to
  cash even with `ACCOUNT_TYPE=margin`. Missing or invalid `ACCOUNT_TYPE`
  also resolves to cash.
* The unit deploy configuration declares the simulator pair. This is code
  preparation, not evidence of a production deployment. Caller `TRD_ENV`
  does not prove broker identity: the REAL bridge independently checks its
  CASH sub-account, sellable holdings and cash coverage before submitting.
* SIMULATE keeps its existing account/ledger. The fixed trading universe,
  RECON, other risk guards and REAL order-enable gate remain in force.
* This supersedes the 2026-10-27 cash-only policy for explicitly configured
  simulator sessions. The NKE short incident recorded in
  [trades](/system/echidna-tables/trades.md) remains historical evidence;
  this clarification does not reclassify or rewrite past trades.

## Ledger and historical incidents

Permitting a new simulator short changes future order eligibility, not
historical rows or account attribution. New broker fills follow the
existing signed-quantity RECON calculation; covering BUYs close short
round-trips under the [trades vocabulary](/system/echidna-tables/trades.md).
No historical order is replayed, no reconciliation epoch is reset, and
RECON failure still blocks risk increases. The policy change alone does
not prove ledger integrity; divergence must be investigated against broker
fills before further risk increases.

The linked NKE incident is documented there as the 2026-10-07 840-share
short (sell 332723, cover 332758), with a known opening-leg classification
left unchanged because both legs are terminal and net to zero. Simulator
permission going forward does not retroactively approve that incident or
authorize a data correction. Configuration/test commands and the
unverified live-deployment status are recorded in the
[cash constraint](/system/constitution/cash-account-constraint.md).

# Behaviour

The guard uses `isHeldPosition()`, which checks a session-scoped set in
`src/llm.js` (`heldSymbols`) that is populated when the LLM calls the
`get_positions` tool during the session. New short-entry SELLs are blocked
with `blocked_by: 'cash_account_guard'`; SELLs that close an existing long
position pass through. Caveat: because the held set is populated only by
`get_positions` calls, a session that never queries positions cannot prove a
SELL is an exit — the guard then treats it as a new short and blocks it.

# Constitution basis

[POSITION MANAGEMENT](/system/constitution/position-management.md): cash
accounts may only open with BUY and may SELL to close or reduce held longs.
