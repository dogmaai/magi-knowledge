# Purpose

Keeps HOLD decisions out of the order path. A `place_order` call with a missing
or `<= 0` quantity is rejected instead of being sent to the broker.

# Behaviour

The response instructs the unit to use `log_analysis` with `action=HOLD`
instead. `log_analysis` is required before every order, so HOLD decisions are
recorded as analysis rather than represented by a zero-quantity order.

# Constitution basis

[Thought recording](/system/constitution/thought-recording.md): `log_analysis`
is required before every order and is the correct path for HOLD decisions.
